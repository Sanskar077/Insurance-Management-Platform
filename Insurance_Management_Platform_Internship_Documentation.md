# Insurance Management Platform

Converted from the provided PDF into Markdown for Claude Code.

## Introduction

The Insurance Management Platform is a comprehensive web-based
application for managing customers, policies, claims, premium payments,
documents, and reports.

## Objectives

-   Enterprise web application
-   Role-based authentication
-   REST APIs
-   Relational database
-   File uploads
-   Workflow management
-   Reporting dashboards
-   Validation
-   Deployment

## Modules

### Customer Management

-   Register Customers
-   View Customer Profile
-   Edit Customer Information
-   Search Customers
-   Customer History

### Policy Management

-   Create Policies
-   View Active Policies
-   Renew Policies
-   Cancel Policies
-   Policy Expiry Notifications

### Claim Management

-   Submit Claims
-   Upload Documents
-   Verify Claims
-   Approve / Reject
-   Claim History

### Premium Tracking

-   Premium Payments
-   Due Dates
-   Payment History
-   Overdue Alerts

### Document Management

-   Upload Documents
-   Download Documents
-   View Files

### Reports Dashboard

-   Active Policies
-   Expired Policies
-   Claim Statistics
-   Premium Collection
-   Customer Growth
-   Monthly Reports

## User Roles

### Administrator

-   Manage employees
-   Manage customers
-   Create policies
-   Assign claims
-   Reports
-   System settings

### Insurance Agent

-   Register customers
-   Create policies
-   Verify documents
-   Review claims
-   Approve or reject claims

### Customer

-   Register/Login
-   View policies
-   Download policy documents
-   Pay premiums
-   Upload claim documents
-   Submit claims
-   Track claim status
